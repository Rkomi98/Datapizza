Ask yourselves now
    Six rules

    One real bottleneck

    Pick something that slows your team down today, not a toy problem. Name it in one sentence.

    Plan before code

    Discuss the problem, write the plan and the definition of done. Mirko reviews it at your desk before you build.

    Work in parallel

    Split the plan into parts that touch different files. Each person drives their own Claude Code session on one part, then you merge.

    Evidence, not claims

    Show a test, a before and after number, or a run. «It works» is not enough.

    You stay in charge

    The agent proposes, you review every change. No passwords, tokens or secrets in prompts.

    Every team presents

    5 minutes of pitch, 2 of questions, 1 to hand over, by screen share on Teams. First pitch at 11:45.

    Mirko walks between the teams all morning and looks at what you are building. Stuck for more than 10 minutes? Call him over, don't wait for him to pass.
